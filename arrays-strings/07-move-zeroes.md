## Problem: Move Zeroes (Easy)

**Link:** https://leetcode.com/problems/move-zeroes/

### Approach

I moved all non-zero elements to the front while keeping their original order.
Then I filled the remaining positions with zeros.

### Complexity

- Time: O(n)
- Space: O(1)

### Notes

Tested with a normal case containing zeros and an edge case containing only zeros.
